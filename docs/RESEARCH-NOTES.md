# Research Notes

**Snapshot date:** 28 September 2026  
**Primary target budget:** ₹40–45 lakh  
**Locations:** Vizag, Tanuku, Palakollu, Bhimavaram

## Decision framework

The dashboard considers location/connectivity, gated/community environment, building age, plot ownership or UDS, asking rate versus locality benchmark, construction risk, water/drainage, legal checks, negotiability, and resale/rental utility.

## Location strategy

### Vizag
At ₹40–45L, proper gated-community inventory in the most central belts is constrained. Focus: Sheela Nagar, Sujatha Nagar, PM Palem and Madhurawada.

### Tanuku
Independent houses receive more weight because land ownership can provide better long-term value than a similarly priced flat.

### Palakollu
Online inventory is thin. Independent houses and land-heavy opportunities are preferred. Local/off-market inquiry is likely to outperform portals.

### Bhimavaram
Better residential pockets can be more expensive than Tanuku/Palakollu. Independent houses remain attractive when plot value works; otherwise a sensibly priced gated flat is a reasonable fallback.

## Limitations
- Live/discoverable does not guarantee the unit is unsold.
- Portal area fields can be incorrect.
- Market rates are working references from available listing data.
- Construction quality cannot be established from photos, portal descriptions or social media.
- Deal labels are research heuristics, not professional valuations.
