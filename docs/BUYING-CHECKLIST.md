# Property Buying Checklist

Use this before paying any token advance.

## Legal and ownership
- Verify seller identity and ownership chain.
- Obtain Encumbrance Certificate for an appropriate period.
- Match survey number, boundaries and extent to title documents.
- Verify sanctioned building plan and local-body approvals.
- Verify RERA registration where applicable.
- Check Occupancy / Completion Certificate where applicable.
- Check current property tax and utility dues.
- Confirm the property is bank-loan eligible with a major lender.
- Use an independent property lawyer.

## Independent house / villa
- Confirm exact plot area in square yards.
- Measure actual boundaries and road frontage.
- Confirm road width and whether road is public/approved.
- Check setbacks and deviations from the sanctioned plan.
- Separate land value from depreciated building value.
- Inspect columns, beams, slabs, cracks and unauthorised structural alterations.
- Inspect terrace waterproofing and bathroom seepage.
- Verify bore/municipal water and summer reliability.
- Check drainage, road level and local flooding history.

## Flat / apartment
- Confirm carpet area, built-up area and saleable/super-built-up area.
- Confirm UDS in the sale deed.
- Confirm parking ownership/allotment.
- Review association maintenance, corpus and outstanding dues.
- Check lift age/AMC and generator capacity.
- Ask about water source and tanker dependence.
- Inspect common-area maintenance, terrace and basement.

## Financial
- Compare recent registered transactions nearby where possible.
- Add registration, brokerage, corpus, parking, GST if applicable, interiors and repairs to the all-in cost.
- Negotiate using age, defects, UDS/plot size and immediate repair costs.

## Site visit
- Visit once in daylight and once in the evening.
- Speak with neighbours, association/security and nearby shop owners.
- Test approach road, parking manoeuvrability and noise.
- Check commute to hospital, school, railway/bus routes and daily shopping.
